A LITTLE GUIDE

How to play

Drag or swipe the globe in the direction you want it to move, including down over the poles. Scroll, pinch, or use + and − to zoom in close. Choose a country, then a city from its lights or the list. The sunlit and night sides follow your device clock and date.

Open the guild world map to pan and zoom around a stylized Earth with glowing destinations. Use W or Z to walk forward, S back, Q or the left arrow to move left, and D right. Hold A while moving to run. On a phone, move with the joystick and hold A Run. Drag to turn the camera. Approach a glowing sunflower shrine and press E or Explore to gather its petal.

Change Mindy's look from the companion panel. Walk past the sunflower path and orange trees to the journey gallery to see the places you marked visited. Tap What-If Lantern whenever your thoughts feel crowded. Name a thought if you wish, notice three things around you, and choose a small step or let the thought rest. Nothing is scored or saved unless you choose to keep it in the diary.

Walk to the portal or press M for the map, the pavilion for the diary, or the bench to customize your companion. Find three petals to unlock the chapter choices. There are no losing choices, deadlines, or streaks.
