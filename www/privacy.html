What is saved

If you create an account, we store your email address for sign-in and your game data: diary text, Mindy's selected appearance, named companions and their chosen appearance, visited places, custom places and story progress. The app also keeps a local copy on your device for offline use and stores an authentication session there. The itinerary built into the game contains place names without bookings or exact dates.

How it is used

We use this data to let you play, keep your pages, and sync them across devices using Supabase as our hosting and authentication provider. This version has no ads, analytics, location tracking, camera access, or data sales. The app does not read your precise location.

Retention and deletion

Your synced data stays until you delete your account. You can delete it in Journey settings or at the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes its synced game data. Local browser data can be removed by clearing that browser's site storage or uninstalling the app; exported backups remain under your control.
